Integrity Grove partnered with Peoples Savings Bank to produce a fun and engaging video campaign promoting their latest scratch-off giveaway. The goal? To get the word out that anyone who opens a new account has a chance to win cash and treat themselves to some of their favorite things.

Peoples Savings Bank came to us with a clear vision, and we worked together to bring it to life in a way that felt both local and memorable. The final video blends energy, excitement, and a touch of humor to capture attention and drive new customer engagement.

## Why Did PSB Choose Us?

Peoples Savings Bank continues to partner with Integrity Grove because of the strong creative relationship we’ve built through past projects. For their recent scratch-off campaign, they trusted us to turn their vision into a high-quality video that encourages new account openings with a chance to win cash and enjoy their favorite things. Our ability to deliver quickly without sacrificing creativity makes us a trusted partner for their marketing efforts.

## Services Performed

Our team at Integrity Grove led the full production of Peoples Savings Bank’s scratch-off campaign video. From early planning to final edits, we managed every part of the process: scheduling, directing, filming, and post-production. The result was a fun, polished video that brought their campaign to life and delivered exactly what the PSB team envisioned.

**Pre-Production Planning:** 
We worked closely with Peoples Savings Bank from the start, planning every detail to align the video with their marketing goals. This included developing the script, mapping out shots, and shaping the overall concept to create a fun and engaging campaign that connected with their audience.

**Directing:** 
Our team directed the entire production for Peoples Savings Bank, making sure the tone was fun and light while staying true to the campaign’s concept. We kept everything on schedule and ensured the final video matched their vision.

**Post-Production:** 
We handled all editing and post-production for the Peoples Savings Bank video, making sure it was delivered on time and met the high standards they were looking for. We refined the final cut, added effects, and pulled everything together for a smooth, polished result.
