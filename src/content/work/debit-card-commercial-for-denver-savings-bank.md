Integrity Grove partnered with Denver Savings Bank to create a fun and meaningful video campaign promoting their new debit card program. With each swipe, 5 cents is donated to Waverly Shell Rock schools, turning everyday purchases into real support for local students.

The Denver team had a strong vision for the project, and we worked closely with them to bring it to life in a way that felt personal and community focused. The final video highlights the heart behind the program while keeping it light, engaging, and easy to connect with.

## Why Did DSB Choose Us?

Denver Savings Bank continues to partner with Integrity Grove thanks to the strong creative relationship we’ve built over time. For their recent debit card campaign benefiting Waverly Shell Rock schools, they trusted us to create a high-quality video that shared the story in a way that was clear, heartfelt, and engaging. Our ability to move quickly while maintaining a strong creative focus makes us a reliable partner for their marketing efforts.

## Services Performed

Our team at Integrity Grove led the full production of Denver Savings Bank’s debit card campaign video. From early planning to final edits, we managed every part of the process including scheduling, directing, filming, and post-production. The final product was a polished, engaging video that captured the heart of the program and brought the bank’s vision to life.

**Pre-Production Planning:** 
We worked closely with Denver Savings Bank from the start, planning every detail to make sure the video aligned with their marketing goals. This included writing the script, mapping out shots, and building a concept that told the story of the debit card program in a clear and engaging way.

**Directing:** 
Our team directed the entire production for Denver Savings Bank, making sure the tone stayed light and engaging while clearly communicating the impact of the debit card program. We kept the project on schedule and made sure the final video matched their vision from start to finish.

**Post-Production:** 
We handled all editing and post-production for the Denver Savings Bank video, making sure it was delivered on time and met the high standards they expected. We refined the final cut, added visual touches, and pulled everything together for a smooth, polished result that brought the campaign to life.
