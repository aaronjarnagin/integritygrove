We’ve been working with Catalyst Gym and Clinic in Ames to create consistent, high quality social media content that reflects who they are and the community they serve. Through photo and video, we’re capturing everything from workouts to wellness treatments, giving people an inside look at what makes Catalyst different.

The goal is simple. Show up online in the same way they show up in person. Real, professional, and full of purpose. Each piece of content is made to build trust, highlight results, and tell the story of a gym and clinic that is focused on helping people move and feel better.

Whether it is a quick reel, a testimonial, or a behind the scenes moment, we are helping Catalyst grow their presence with content that actually connects.

## Why Did Catalyst Choose Us?

Catalyst Gym and Clinic partnered with Integrity Grove to bring their story to life through photo and video content that feels true to who they are. They chose us because they wanted a team that could move quickly, communicate clearly, and create content that looks and feels like them. With a focus on both fitness and recovery, Catalyst needed more than generic marketing. They needed visuals that highlight their unique approach, strong community, and commitment to results. We worked closely with their team to capture authentic moments in a way that was both hands on and efficient. This project reflects what we aim to do for every client. Tell real stories with clarity and purpose.
