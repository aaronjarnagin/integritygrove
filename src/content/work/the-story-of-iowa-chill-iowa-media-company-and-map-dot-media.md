In this video, we sit down with the team behind Iowa Chill and Map Dot Media to explore how it all began. What started as a passion project highlighting Midwest culture quickly turned into a brand with a massive following. That momentum led to the launch of Map Dot Media, a company built to help other businesses create meaningful content and grow their online presence.

You’ll hear firsthand how the brand evolved, what challenges they faced along the way, and how they continue to bring storytelling and strategy together for clients across the state of Iowa.

Whether you’re a fan of Iowa Chill or curious about the journey from idea to impact, this behind-the-scenes look is one you won’t want to miss.

## Why Did Map Dot Media Choose Us?

Map Dot Media partnered with Integrity Grove to help tell their story, from the early days of Iowa Chill to the growth of a full-service creative agency. They trusted us to produce a video that felt true to who they are: creative, authentic, and deeply rooted in Iowa culture. By working closely with their founders, we crafted a piece that captures their journey and vision while keeping the production process efficient and focused. It’s the kind of collaboration that reflects what we do best, telling real stories with heart and clarity.

## Services Performed

Our team at Integrity Grove led the full production of Map Dot Media’s brand story video. From the early planning stages to final edits, we handled every part of the process including scheduling, directing, filming, and post-production. The final result was a polished and authentic video that captured their journey from Iowa Chill to a growing creative agency, highlighting the heart behind their work and the people who built it.

**Pre-Production Planning:** 
We worked closely with Denver Savings Bank from the start, planning every detail to make sure the video aligned with their marketing goals. This included writing the script, mapping out shots, and building a concept that told the story of the debit card program in a clear and engaging way.

**Directing:** Our team directed the entire production for Map Dot Media, making sure the tone felt true to their brand while clearly communicating their growth and vision. We kept the project on schedule and ensured the final video reflected their story from start to finish.

**Post-Production:** We handled all editing and post-production for the Map Dot Media video, making sure it was delivered on time and met the high standards they were looking for. We refined the final cut, added visual touches, and brought everything together to create a smooth and polished result that captured the heart of their story.
