Integrity Grove partnered with Jyles FBA to grow organic traffic and boost sales for his Amazon FBA community. Our goal was to enhance his online presence and attract new customers through strategic content marketing and SEO efforts.

![Jyles FBA: overview, image 1](/images/work/growing-a-saas-brand-through-social-media-seo/screenshot-2024-05-31-at-2-45-49-pm.webp)

## Why Did Jyles FBA Choose Us?

Having seen our consistent and effective content strategies in the past, Jyles trusted us to deliver the same level of excellence for his brand. Our proven track record in social media and content marketing made us the ideal choice.

## Services Performed

To elevate Jyles FBA’s online presence and drive organic traffic, we implemented a multi-faceted strategy. This included creating engaging short-form content across social platforms, writing keyword-targeted articles, and integrating multi-funnel CTAs to attract new customers. Additionally, we built and integrated a profit calculator into one of the articles to provide valuable tools for potential customers. This comprehensive approach ensured rapid growth and increased engagement across all channels.

**Content Strategy:** We developed a targeted content strategy, creating short-form content across all social platforms and writing articles focused on specific keywords to drive organic traffic.

![Jyles FBA: services performed, image 1](/images/work/growing-a-saas-brand-through-social-media-seo/img-0527.webp)

**Engaging Posts:** 
Our team produced engaging posts designed to attract and retain followers, with multi-funnel CTAs to convert views into customers.

![Jyles FBA: services performed, image 2](/images/work/growing-a-saas-brand-through-social-media-seo/img-0557.webp)

**SEO Optimization:** 
We optimized the website for search engines, focusing on keywords relevant to the Amazon FBA community, leading to significant improvements in organic search rankings.

![Jyles FBA: services performed, image 3](/images/work/growing-a-saas-brand-through-social-media-seo/img-0558.webp)

## Outcomes and Impact

Our content production and social media management efforts yielded over 900,000 views on TikTok, 160,000 views on YouTube, and more than 79,000 search appearances, along with over 2,900 new users, in just over a year on our brand-new website.

Additionally, from our article generation and SEO efforts, Jyles FBA’s website **searches grew an average of 93%**  month over month for the first four months, and **clicks grew by an average of 165%** month over month.
