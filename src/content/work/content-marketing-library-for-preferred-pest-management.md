Integrity Grove partnered with Preferred Pest Management (PPM) to create a visual brand experience that reflects their trusted, people-first approach to pest control. These visuals give Preferred Pest Management professional content they can use across their website, ads, and social media while staying true to their local identity and reputation. This project will be featured on integritygrove.com as an example of how we help service-based businesses build trust through authentic media.

## Services Performed

Creating the visual assets for Preferred Pest Management included three key phases: pre‑planning, filming and photography, and post‑production. Each step was designed to align with their brand and give them a professional media library they can use online and in digital campaigns.

**Pre-Planning:** We met with the Preferred Pest Management team to understand their story, values, and goals for the project. We built a clear shot list for both the website header video and photography, focusing on team interaction, service in the field, and their commitment to customer care. This direction ensured every part of production ran efficiently and stayed on brand.

**Filming & Photography:** Our team spent time on-site capturing real interactions and service moments, along with clean brand visuals for the website. We balanced action shots with team portraits to show both expertise and personality. The sessions ran smoothly around their normal schedule, so operations could continue without disruption.

**Post-Production:** We edited the website header video for polished pacing, color, and clarity while keeping the tone genuine. The photo collection was edited for consistency across lighting, color, and brand feel. The final assets give Preferred Pest Management a versatile library to use across their website, ads, and social media.
