Integrity Grove partnered with Denver Savings Bank to create engaging and nostalgic video content aimed at promoting their unique offerings in a fun and memorable way. Their team came to us with some fantastic creative ideas already in mind, and it was a blast to bring those concepts to life.

We used themes like a “Saved by the Bell”-inspired throwback to highlight their debit card donation program, combining humor and community impact. Another project involved a humorous commercial showing a customer frustrated by a robotic phone answering system, emphasizing the importance of personalized service.

## Why Did DSB Choose Us?

Denver Savings Bank chose Integrity Grove because of our proven expertise in delivering creative and high-quality video production for past clients. We demonstrated our ability to take their existing ideas and bring them to life, aligning with their vision while adding our unique flair. Our track record of producing successful, engaging videos in tight timelines showed DSB that we had the skills to execute their projects efficiently, while still maintaining a high standard of quality and creativity, making us the perfect fit for their marketing needs.

## Services Performed

Our team at Integrity Grove handled the full scope of production for Denver Savings Bank’s video projects, ensuring smooth and efficient execution. From meticulous pre-production planning, directing the shoots to capture the nostalgic themes, and managing the fast-paced filming schedule, to post-production work that polished the videos to perfection, we ensured everything was delivered on time and exceeded expectations.

**Pre-Production Planning:** 
We worked closely with Denver Savings Bank from the start, planning every detail to ensure the creative vision was aligned with their marketing goals. This included script development, shot planning, and theme conceptualization, all focused on delivering a unique and engaging message.

**Directing:** 
Our team directed the entire production, ensuring that the nostalgic elements and humor came through authentically while keeping the production on track and aligned with deadlines.

**Post-Production:** 
Integrity Grove handled all aspects of editing and post-production, making sure the videos were completed on time and met the high-quality standards expected by the client. This included refining the final cut, adding effects, and ensuring a seamless delivery.
